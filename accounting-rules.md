ACCOUNTING: DEBITS & CREDITS

The basic accounting equation:

ASSETS = LIABILITIES + EQUITY

Debit = LEFT
Credit = RIGHT

Therefore:

LEFT / DEBIT
• Increase an asset = Debit
• Increase an expense = Debit

RIGHT / CREDIT
• Increase a liability = Credit
• Increase equity = Credit
• Increase revenue = Credit

Why?

Revenue increases equity → Credit
Expenses decrease equity → Debit

Expanded equation:

ASSETS + EXPENSES = LIABILITIES + EQUITY + REVENUE

So the intuitive rule is:

DEBIT (left): Assets + Expenses
CREDIT (right): Liabilities + Equity + Revenue

To decrease any account, do the opposite.

The key idea:
Don't think "debit = bad" or "credit = good."
Debit simply means LEFT.
Credit simply means RIGHT.
